    Game setup

    Game setup
    Magic and Commander only. Players 1 and 2 are a team, then 3 and 4, and so on; change turn order to change teams. Teammates share one life total and one turn, and either can pass. Needs an even number of players, at least 4. The starting team skips its first draw.

    Off: no countdown; a gentle cue appears after 5 minutes. With a timer, Atlas warns 10 seconds before time runs out. Running out of time never passes the turn.

    Any seated player can change these in the lobby.

    Life and Commander damage do not automatically eliminate a player. Changes to another player's life need their approval; Atlas accepts unanswered requests after 15 seconds, or the longer time a player chose.

            Paired Sigils

            Pair new Sigils with Menu → Pair a Sigil on the Atlas screen and the Sigil’s Pair button. Forget works in the lobby, for Sigils nobody is seated on.

            Holding a Sigil’s own Pair button for 10 seconds makes the Sigil forget Atlas.

            Atlas hardware

            How long Atlas listens after Pair a Sigil is tapped on its screen (under Menu): at least 60 seconds, longer for players who need more time. A Sigil listens for 60 seconds, so with a longer window tap Pair on Atlas first, then press Pair on the Sigil.

            Atlas's own speaker plays table-wide cues: the start countdown, turn changes, turn-timer warnings, pause and resume, eliminations, wins and game over, so players without a Sigil hear them too. Every cue also shows on screen. Sigil sound is set per player in their accessibility settings.
